# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Players and Dungeon Masters use the site during preparation and active play. They need to locate game information quickly, build and manage characters, and run sessions without interface friction or visual distraction.

## Product Purpose

5etools makes playing tabletop roleplaying games with friends easier through a broad suite of searchable rules references, character tools, and DM utilities. Success means that both audiences can complete their task confidently and efficiently, even when the interface presents substantial game data.

## Brand Personality

Practical, familiar, unobtrusive. The interface should feel dependable and information-dense without competing with the game or its users' attention.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative SaaS styling, novelty-first interactions, and visual treatments that reduce scanability or make tool workflows feel less familiar.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the game and the task in focus, not the interface.
2. Make dense information easy to scan, filter, and act on.
3. Preserve familiar, predictable controls across tools.
4. Favor dependable feedback and clear states over visual novelty.
5. Support both preparation and in-session use under time pressure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA. Preserve readable contrast, keyboard operation, clear focus, reduced-motion support, and interfaces that remain usable for color-blind users.
