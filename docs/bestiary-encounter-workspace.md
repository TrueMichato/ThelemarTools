# Encounter Workspace

The separate [Encounter Workspace](../encounterworkspace.html) starts with a **saved Bestiary pinned list**, not the current pins or an uploaded list. Open it from the Bestiary's control bar (or its Encounter Builder settings when active) or the Dungeon Master navigation menu, then choose a saved list. A working copy is stored in this browser; neither the saved Bestiary list nor the DM Screen NPC Manager roster is changed.

Each counted creature becomes its own instance with a stable ID. Scaled CR, spell-summon, and class-summon variants are resolved using the pinned list's custom hash before a statblock snapshot is copied for each instance. The snapshot stays unchanged even if the Bestiary source or saved list later changes. All targets start selected; the roster checkboxes and **All/None** controls save individual target selection. Reloading restores the working encounter and selection without looking up the original source again.

Choosing another saved list replaces the working copy only after confirmation (including when the current roster is empty). Missing homebrew, invalid scaling, and invalid counts are reported individually; available monsters still load. If no entries resolve, the encounter remains a named empty working copy with the load notices. The workspace does not write to Bestiary saved-list storage, and choosing or cancelling a list does not affect current pins.

To protect the browser from an unbounded roster, a saved list can materialize at most 1,000 instances. An entry that would exceed this limit is reported as omitted rather than partially materialized.

## Rolls and conditions

Above the roster, choose an **ability check**, **saving throw**, or **skill check**, then an ability/skill and Normal, Advantage, or Disadvantage. **Roll selected** rolls once for each selected instance through the site's dice roller and displays each monster's die, bonus, total, and roll effect. Saving throws and skills use explicit monster bonuses where present, otherwise their governing ability modifier; installed skill definitions contribute their governing ability when available. Duplicate monsters are listed and rolled independently. Cancelling a roll or receiving an invalid dice result produces a failure row, not a made-up total; other selected monsters still complete.

**Conditions** applies/removes a chosen condition on the selected instances only. Each statblock shows its own removable condition chips, and the roster summarizes them. The picker uses site conditions and installed homebrew where available; conditions already in an encounter remain visible and removable if their source later disappears. Hover a known chip for its definition. Normal 5e condition effects supported by the NPC Manager apply to these checks and saves: Poisoned and Frightened disadvantage on checks (including skills), Restrained disadvantage on Dexterity saves, and automatic failure of Strength and Dexterity saves for Paralyzed, Stunned, Unconscious, and Petrified. A chosen advantage and condition disadvantage cancel (and vice versa); the result explains why. Conditional effects are shown with the NPC Manager's assumptions, such as Frightened's source being in sight. No custom homebrew mechanics are inferred from condition text.

If condition/skill references cannot load, the page reports the error and keeps standard fallback choices available; saved Bestiary list import remains enabled when its sources initialized successfully.

Conditions and target selection are saved only on the encounter instances; the frozen monster snapshots and saved Bestiary list remain unchanged. Foundation v1 encounters load with empty conditions; check/save/skill rolls and their result table are transient, not persisted.

## Area notes and roll modifiers

Expand **Area effects** beneath the roll controls when editing traits, notes, or modifiers; collapse it to keep the roster close to the roll controls during play. Use **Area traits & lair notes** to give selected monsters a named, plain-text trait or a **manual lair-action note**. Notes are reminders only: no lair action triggers or dice are automated. A note applied to several targets is shown on every affected statblock, with a per-monster Remove button. Choose a note from **Applied note** to remove it from currently selected monsters in bulk; unselected instances, including duplicates of the same creature, keep their own copy. DM-authored names and descriptions are displayed as text, not parsed as HTML or game tags.

**Area roll modifiers** offers two different Desecrated Ground rules. **DMG p. 110** gives an undead creature standing there advantage on *saving throws only*. The labeled **house rule** gives an undead creature standing there advantage on *saving throws and ability checks, including skill checks*. The picker describes the chosen rule before application. Select only monsters standing in the affected area; there is no map or inferred range. An applied preset skips selected non-undead monsters by their actual monster type (`type: "undead"` or `type: {type: "undead", ...}`) and reports their names/count in the status message. An unselected undead is not affected. Applying the other preset to an eligible instance replaces its previous Desecrated Ground variant, not its separate DM-authored modifiers. Removing the preset uses its per-statblock Remove button or the **Applied modifier** bulk picker.

For other situations, give a **custom roll modifier** a name/source, choose checks (which also covers skills), saves, or both, choose advantage/disadvantage/no mode change, and enter a signed whole-number bonus (zero is valid with advantage or disadvantage). This is deliberately not an arbitrary rules engine; a normal-mode zero-bonus entry does nothing and is rejected. Bonuses from separate sources add to the monster's original roll bonus, without changing the statblock. Named advantage and disadvantage combine with existing conditions and the manually selected roll mode; any one of each cancels. Automatic condition failures still take priority. Each completed result shows its effects and flat-bonus sources; cancelled or invalid dice remain explicit failures. Choose an **Applied modifier** to remove it from selected targets in bulk, or use a single statblock's Remove button. Only the selected instance records are updated, so two identical monsters can have different effects.

## Hit points and initiative

Each statblock now has independent **Current HP**, **Maximum HP**, **Temp HP**, and **Initiative** fields, including duplicate creatures. Current and maximum start from the monster's numeric `hp.average` (including zero). If the source has no usable numeric average, both are explicitly **Unset**, not zero; enter values directly. Temp starts at zero. Direct edits are saved independently, and a failed edit is restored on screen with an error. The roster displays each instance's own HP and initiative alongside its conditions/effects. The authored monster statblock is not rewritten when HP changes.

**Selected monsters' HP** accepts a bare number or dice expression as damage, `-` as damage, `+` as healing, or `=` as a set value. For example, `8d6`, `-30`, `+12`, and `=15` all use the same selected targets. One dice expression is evaluated **once per batch** and that result is applied to every eligible instance. **Half damage** halves damage and rounds its magnitude down; it does not halve healing or an absolute set. Damage uses temporary HP first, then current HP (minimum zero); healing cannot raise current above maximum. A set value can exceed maximum. Monsters with unknown maximum HP are skipped; damage/healing also skips unknown current HP. The status names/counts skipped instances, and no dice are evaluated when no selected monster is eligible. **Undo HP** restores the last committed bulk operation, up to five recent operations in this page session; it is cleared on a direct HP edit or encounter replacement, and not restored after a page reload. A failed apply or undo does not change saved HP or consume an undo.

**Roll selected initiative** uses each monster's authored initiative bonus (including explicit monster bonuses) and rolls through the site's dice roller. Initiative is a Dexterity *ability check* for Poisoned/Frightened and check-scoped manual or area effects; the monster's authored initiative advantage also participates. Cancelled, unavailable, or invalid rolls do not overwrite an existing total. Successful selected rolls are saved together, with per-monster results and failures reported. Initiative may also be entered/cleared manually on each statblock.

**Turn order** lists rolled monsters in descending total order; equal totals keep original roster order. Unrolled monsters are listed as such, not inserted in the order. **Start turns** explicitly starts round 1 with the first rolled monster; **Next turn** advances to the next monster and wraps to the first in a new round. **Reset turns** clears the active turn and returns to round zero without erasing initiative. Changing an initiative total reorders entries but preserves the currently active monster; clearing that monster's initiative safely resets turns. Round and active monster survive a reload.

Working encounters load as **v4**: v1 gains empty conditions/notes/modifiers, v2 retains conditions and gains empty notes/modifiers, and v3 retains all its effects. Each legacy version gains independent HP, unrolled initiative, and an unstarted turn without rewriting storage on load. Replacing the encounter asks for confirmation and discards its HP, initiative, turns, conditions, area notes, modifiers, and target selection. A failed save leaves visible persisted state unchanged. The Bestiary saved list, pinned monsters, frozen snapshots, and DM Screen NPC Manager/Initiative Tracker state remain independent.

## One-time DM Screen handoff

Select the monsters to send and enter or roll an initiative total for **every** selected instance. **Queue selected monsters** creates a one-time snapshot in this browser. If one or more selected monsters have no initiative, the page names each missing instance and does not queue a partial roster. If a different snapshot is already queued, replacing it requires confirmation; **Clear queued snapshot** also requires confirmation. The queue displays the saved list name, monster count, and creation time. You can queue monsters while DM Screen is closed or has no Initiative Tracker panel yet.

Open DM Screen, add an **Initiative Tracker** panel if necessary, then use **Refresh queue** and **Import here** on the specific panel that should receive the monsters. Nothing imports automatically. That panel appends separate rows with each instance's name/number, frozen statblock, current/maximum/temporary HP (including unset current or maximum), initiative total, and standard or custom condition names. Locked or failed imports leave the queue available; after successful, verified persistence the queue is cleared. If the rows are visible but saving or queue clearing fails, reload DM Screen before trying again rather than importing duplicates. Each panel has its own **Clear queue** action; clearing never removes rows already imported. An unreadable queue cannot be replaced; use the separately confirmed **Clear damaged queue** recovery action instead.

The source working encounter, selected targets, Bestiary saved list, and pins are not modified. **Later HP, conditions, initiative, and turns never sync in either direction.** Area notes, manual lair-action reminders, and custom roll modifiers remain in Encounter Workspace because Initiative Tracker has no equivalent fields; they are disclosed before queuing/importing. The handoff uses same-origin browser storage and requires persistent storage and cross-tab locking. If those are unavailable, it reports the problem rather than claiming that a queue will survive reload. Directly editing DM Screen data in a second tab can still overwrite another tab's board state; close or reload stale tabs before continuing.

Attacks, action economy, maps, and automatic lair actions remain out of scope.
