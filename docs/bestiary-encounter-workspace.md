# Encounter Workspace

The separate [Encounter Workspace](../encounterworkspace.html) can start directly from the **active Bestiary Encounter Builder**, even if it has not been saved: click **Encounter Workspace** in its settings. The same-tab handoff copies the current roster, counts, and scaled/summoned variants, plus the active saved list's name when present. An unsaved encounter is called "Current Bestiary Encounter." The Bestiary encounter and saved lists are not changed. Outside Encounter Builder, the Bestiary control bar and Dungeon Master navigation menu still open the workspace normally; use **Choose saved Bestiary list** there to load a named list instead.

If a working encounter already exists, confirm before replacing it. Declining leaves it intact. An empty, unreadable, partially resolvable, or stale Bestiary handoff does **not** replace the working copy: the page explains what failed and asks you to return to Bestiary and try again (or choose a saved list). The handoff is consumed once and removed from the address bar, so reloading or revisiting the page restores only the working copy; it does not import the roster a second time.

Each counted creature becomes its own instance with a stable ID. Scaled CR, spell-summon, and class-summon variants are resolved using the pinned list's custom hash before a statblock snapshot is copied for each instance. The snapshot stays unchanged even if the Bestiary source or saved list later changes. All targets start selected; the roster checkboxes and **All/None** controls save individual target selection. Reloading restores the working encounter, edits, and selection without looking up the original source again.

**Award XP** beside the working list name is the live, *unadjusted* reward total for all encounter instances, independent of selected targets, defeated status, or roster filters. Each rendered statblock also shows its own award XP. It is calculated from the **effective** creature after saved statblock edits and transformations, honoring an explicit `cr.xp` override, including `xp: 0`; a normal CR 0 without an override yields 10 XP. An unrated/unsupported CR or invalid XP override is reported as unknown, not silently treated as zero; a mixed roster shows the known subtotal and rated/total count (the unrated creatures are excluded). These are reward XP, **not** the encounter-building adjusted XP or difficulty multiplier. No estimate changes either CR or XP.

Open **Assess viewed creature's CR · 2014 advisory** below the statblock to compare the original and effective CR, HP, AC and changed mechanical fields, see the saved edit count, text-only area/lair notes, and any transformation manual-review requirements. For a *2014 DMG table* estimate, the DM must confirm or adjust effective HP and AC, enter expected **total damage over three rounds** (including expected uses of reactions, auras, recharge abilities, legendary and lair actions), and choose and enter the attack bonus or save DC that best represents its offense. A plain HP/AC is offered only as a starting value when unambiguous; the tool does not parse prose or assume a frequency, saving-throw effect, resistance adjustment, or damage amount. Missing, non-whole, or out-of-table inputs give an error rather than a fabricated CR. The table compares HP/AC for defensive CR and damage/attack or save DC for offensive CR, then averages them; it is an **advisory approximation**, not a universal rating for other editions. Changing the viewed creature or its statblock clears the draft; no assumptions or estimates are saved. To adopt an estimate, explicitly edit the creature's CR in **Edit viewed statblock** and review any separate custom XP override.

During play, the combat strip shows the current round/active turn and HP/conditions, the next turn, and one primary **Start turns** or **Next turn** action. **Order & initiative** opens the canonical numeric order directly below it, including active/next labels, a single entry for each shared turn, and a separate unrolled list. Each entry offers **Edit**, draggable **Move**, and keyboard/touch **Move up/down** buttons; the position picker, one-step **Undo**, **Reset turns**, and selected initiative rolls live in this disclosure. The focused effective statblock follows the combat strip before the roster. Use **Jump to statblock** to choose any encounter instance, including one hidden by the roster filter; **Previous / Next** step through visible instances, and **View active turn** returns to the current combatant. The grouped roster's **View** controls jump directly to any member and support Up/Down arrow navigation. Viewing never selects a target or advances combat; explicit focus survives roster searches and sorting, while advancing a turn focuses the new active member. **Edit viewed statblock** opens that creature's editor directly. The separate roster shows HP, conditions, active/viewing labels and group selection counts.

The focus bar shows the **selected targets** (up to two names inline and a count of the rest), not merely the viewed or active monster. **Damage selected** opens the existing HP form and focuses its expression; **Roll selected saves** opens the existing roll form set to saves and focuses its ability picker. Both apply only to saved selected IDs, never automatically to the viewed or active monster. Expand **Show every selected target** in this form to inspect all names before acting, including targets outside the roster filter. Results, including per-creature cancelled/invalid rolls, appear beside the focused statblock. **Move turn** opens the existing initiative order and position picker, preselecting the viewed monster's entry (or its shared turn) without advancing combat; choose a destination, then use the guarded **Undo** if needed. A shared turn is one entry in this picker, not one move per member. **More cockpit tools** offers the bulk-edit jump, target/condition controls, and roster jump. The roster's **Target & condition tools** includes **Select active turn** (adds the whole shared turn if applicable) and **Select viewed statblock** (adds only the viewed instance). On small screens the order is combat strip, collapsed initiative, focus shortcuts, focused statblock, grouped roster, collapsed condition tools, then advanced actions and DM Screen handoff. The roster has no inner vertical scroller, and opening a tool alone changes no saved state.

The roster search matches name (including numbered duplicates), source, CR, and condition; filters show conditioned, below-half-HP, defeated, or unrolled monsters. A creature renamed by a statblock edit displays its effective name alongside its original numbered label; Name sort and search use the edited name, while turn and handoff labels retain the original identity. Sort by saved source order, descending initiative or CR, ascending current HP or HP percentage, name, or urgent status. Unset HP, unknown or invalid CR and unrolled initiative sort after known values; ties keep the original source order. Groups remain intact under filtering, with the header showing its full count and any smaller shown count. Expand a group to check exact named members; **Select shown (N)** and **Clear shown (N)** affect only members matching the current search/filter, preserving hidden targets. The group checkbox explicitly targets the *entire group*, including filtered-out members, and shows a mixed state when only some are selected. The full targeted count, shown count and no-match message make filters explicit. These are local view choices: IDs, saved targets, persistent groups, ties in canonical initiative, and the active turn never change when searching or sorting.

**Statblocks: Focused / All (load in batches)** changes only the view. Focused is the default and keeps the chosen creature available even if it falls outside the current roster filter. All shows the filtered roster's statblocks in batches of twelve, with separate expandable cards and **Focus** shortcuts. Closed cards do not render their full statblock; opening one loads its own effective edits, HP, conditions, notes/effects, and roll links, and closing it removes that full rendering. **Load more statblocks** reveals the next twelve card shells; a 1,000-instance encounter does not eagerly render 1,000 full statblocks.

## Combat resources

Explicit counted uses and Ready/Spent recharge controls appear **beside the matching ability heading inside the rendered statblock**. Spell-slot pips sit beside their spell level in the spellcasting list. The tiny, close-packed dots wrap into short rows and use a restrained creature-type accent in both themes: filled means available, slashed outline means spent. They are indicators, not individual targets; the adjacent keyboard-labelled, touch-sized **Spend one (−)** and **Restore one (+)** buttons change the count. Counts above twelve show a numeric count and the same buttons instead of dots. Unmatched resources keep the twelve-dot limit in the manager and show an explicit count. **Start concentration** remains a quick toggle in the statblock's top bar; it does not require opening the resource manager. The Ready/Spent button is compact but remains separately labelled and keyboard accessible; clicking a statblock dice link never changes a resource.

The **Manage counts and names** disclosure follows the rendered statblock. It still edits all remaining/maximum counts, adds or removes a spell level, adds/renames/removes a limited-use ability, and labels an active concentration effect. Manually added resources, equipment charges described in prose, and saved counters whose ability or spell level cannot be uniquely matched to the current effective statblock remain usable in a small tracker there rather than attaching to a similarly named ability. The disclosure stays open during updates, and a failed save leaves unsaved entries available for retry. Each counted monster has its own controls, including identical creatures on a shared turn. The roster, active-turn strip, and expanded-card summaries still show a compact snapshot (up to three resources and a remaining count); concentration appears first when active.

New instances start with only **explicit** statblock counts: numeric spell slots, numbered `/Day` ability names, numeric charges in special equipment, explicitly numbered legendary actions, and tagged recharge abilities. Unqualified legendary-action/resistance prose, daily spell lists, and ambiguous descriptions do not imply a maximum; add a manual counter if the source has no explicit count. A recharge link roll does not change the readiness marker. Clicking a statblock action does not spend a use; turns never refill spell slots or limited-use abilities. Concentration does not add/remove conditions or end itself; mark it on or off explicitly.

After a **successfully saved** Start turns or Next turn transition, if the active creature has spent recharge abilities, the page asks **Roll** or **Skip**. A shared initiative turn checks every member independently. Roll uses the site's dice roller to roll `1d6` for each spent ability; a whole-number result at least its displayed threshold (for example, 5 or 6 for Recharge 5–6) restores **only that ability** after the result is saved. The page reports each result, including unsuccessful or cancelled rolls. Skip, dismiss, invalid rolls and failed saves leave readiness unchanged. Expand **Order & initiative → Recharge settings** to enable automatic rolls instead of prompts; this choice is stored separately in the browser and applies across encounters and reloads. Its default is off. Restoring a page, resetting turns, or failing to save a turn never prompts or rolls.

Resources are stored per instance in the browser with the working encounter, not in the frozen monster snapshot or saved Bestiary list. Older encounters initialize independent resource defaults **in memory** on load without rewriting storage; saving a change persists the new format. A later statblock transformation preserves current/spent uses rather than silently recalculating them. If its effective abilities or maximum counts change, edit the counters manually. Invalid saved resource data is rejected instead of being silently corrected, and a failed save leaves the existing working state intact.

The roster visually groups creatures with identical **effective statblocks**, including per-instance Quick Actions edits. Group identity does not depend on monster names, sources, or operation IDs: different edits that produce the same statblock group together, while different resulting statblocks separate. A group header gives the member count, an expand/collapse control, and a checkbox that selects or deselects all members; when only some are selected it shows a mixed state. Individual roster rows, IDs, labels, selection, HP, conditions, and statblock cards remain separate. Use **Split** on a member to keep it separate, **Rejoin** to restore compatible grouping, **Group selected** to persist a chosen subset of identical statblocks, or **Disband** to separate a persistent group. Splitting and persistent group membership survive reload; expand/collapse is a local view preference.

Choosing another saved list replaces the working copy only after confirmation (including when the current roster is empty). Missing homebrew, invalid scaling, and invalid counts are reported individually; available monsters still load. If no entries resolve, the encounter remains a named empty working copy with the load notices. Direct handoffs from the active Bestiary builder require every creature to resolve; they never replace the working copy with a partial roster. The workspace does not write to Bestiary saved-list storage, and choosing or cancelling a list does not affect current pins.

To protect the browser from an unbounded roster, a saved list can materialize at most 1,000 instances. An entry that would exceed this limit is reported as omitted rather than partially materialized.

## Rolls and conditions

Under **Damage & rolls** beside the focused statblock, choose an **ability check**, **saving throw**, or **skill check**, then an ability/skill and Normal, Advantage, or Disadvantage. The target summary above the focused statblock lists selected names, including a count outside the roster's current filter; when selection is empty, Apply/Roll is disabled without disabling navigation. **Roll selected** already applies saved roll effects: it rolls once for each selected instance through the site's dice roller and displays each monster's die, bonus, total, and contributing sources. Saving throws and skills use explicit monster bonuses where present, otherwise their governing ability modifier; installed skill definitions contribute their governing ability when available. Duplicate monsters are listed and rolled independently. Cancelling a roll or receiving an invalid dice result produces a failure row, not a made-up total; other selected monsters still complete.

**Conditions** applies/removes a chosen condition on the selected instances only. Each statblock shows its own removable condition chips, and the roster summarizes them. The picker uses site conditions and installed homebrew where available; conditions already in an encounter remain visible and removable if their source later disappears. Hover a known chip for its definition. Supported condition effects include Poisoned disadvantage on checks and attacks, Restrained disadvantage on Dexterity saves and attacks, and automatic failure of Strength and Dexterity saves for Paralyzed, Stunned, Unconscious, and Petrified. Encounter rolls ask whether Frightened's source is in sight and, for an Invisible attack, whether the target cannot see the attacker. If the prompt is dismissed, no die is rolled; answering No omits that conditional effect. A chosen advantage and condition disadvantage cancel (and vice versa); the result names both. No custom homebrew mechanics are inferred from condition text.

If condition/skill references cannot load, the page reports the error and keeps standard fallback choices available; saved Bestiary list import remains enabled when its sources initialized successfully.

Conditions and target selection are saved only on the encounter instances; the frozen monster snapshots and saved Bestiary list remain unchanged. Foundation v1 encounters load with empty conditions; check/save/skill rolls and their result table are transient, not persisted.

## Area notes and roll effects

Expand **Non-mechanical notes & roll effects** inside the selected actions; collapse it after preparation. Use **Area & lair reminders (text only)** to give selected monsters a named, plain-text area reminder or a **manual lair-action note**. These legacy notes remain non-mechanical reminders: they do not alter stats, appear as actions, trigger lair actions, or automate dice, and they are not converted into mechanical edits when an older encounter loads. A note applied to several targets is shown on every affected statblock, with a per-monster Remove button. Choose a note from **Applied note** to remove it from currently selected monsters in bulk; unselected instances, including duplicates of the same creature, keep their own copy. DM-authored names and descriptions are displayed as text, not parsed as HTML or game tags.

## Mechanical statblock edits

Use **Edit statblock** on a creature to open the same Quick Actions editor used by the Bestiary. Minion conversion, mechanical area traits, lair-action groups, magic items, and Quick Edit (core statistics and guided or Advanced JSON entries, including legendary and mythic actions) are available. Changes are saved as an operation history for **that instance**, not as an override keyed by monster name. Two copies of the same creature can have different edits; Remove and Clear All undo only the chosen instance's saved operations. The copied source snapshot and Bestiary saved list/pins never change. **Save to Homebrew** is separate and writes homebrew only if you explicitly choose it.

Expand **Bulk statblock edits** inside the advanced selected-target actions to add a legendary action, choose a loaded lair-action group, apply an actual area trait, or convert to minions. The preview names the selected rule, lists eligible monsters, and names each skipped monster with its reason (for example, an existing action or an unsupported minion CR). Rule choices use their name and source, so reordering loaded homebrew cannot substitute another rule; a rule that disappears before preview is rejected instead. If confirmed, all eligible changes are saved in one write; a failed write leaves every target unchanged. If a save succeeds but refreshing the page fails, the notice says the changes were saved and asks you to reload rather than inviting a duplicate retry. A mechanical trait can modify the rendered statblock's speed, senses, defenses, HP, or entries according to its rule; unlike the free-text reminders above, these edits affect the creature the encounter actually uses.

**Edit statblock → Templates** uses the same transformation picker and before/after statblock preview for one instance. For a selection, open **Bulk statblock edits → Transform selected monsters**. Choose required recipe options and acknowledge prerequisites; the preview evaluates **each selected instance separately** against its own effective statblock and operation history. It names eligible targets, exact ineligible skips (including full per-instance edit histories or oversized operations), per-target conflicts and winner choices, mechanical diffs, and any HP resets or persistent-group splits. A capped target cannot prevent another eligible target from saving. Confirm unresolved manual-review tasks separately before applying. The selection, target histories, working encounter, and catalog are checked again when you apply; stale previews fail instead of changing a different set. One save publishes all eligible operations atomically. Existing shared turns and individual initiative follow the same group-split rules as other mechanical edits; rendered and rolled statblocks and the v1 DM Screen handoff use the effective transformed result. The source snapshots and Bestiary overrides are never changed by these encounter actions.

When an edit changes the creature's effective `hp.average`, **that instance's** tracked current and maximum HP reset to the new usable numeric average (or Unset if none); temporary HP is retained. The confirmation/status reports the reset, and incompatible in-page HP undo entries are discarded. Other edits do not reset tracked HP. Rendered statblocks, batch and clicked-link rolls, skill options, and a one-time DM Screen handoff use the edited creature, while encounter roll-effect presets and notes remain separate.

**Roll effects** has a searchable, cited preset picker. Search a rule, book code, page, or description. A preset acts only on selected instances; choose only monsters that meet its stated area/item eligibility. The two Desecrated Ground variants skip non-undead by actual monster type and report skipped targets. Applying one replaces only the other Desecrated Ground variant, while other presets and custom effects can coexist. Presets with uncertain context prompt separately *at roll time* (Yes applies, No skips, dismissing cancels that instance's roll); applying the preset does not assert that a ranged attack, weather condition, or orbiting item is always active.

For other situations, give a **custom roll effect** a name/source, choose ability checks (the legacy broad scope also covers skills and initiative), skill checks only, saves, initiative only, and/or attacks; choose advantage/disadvantage/no mode change and enter a signed whole-number bonus (zero is valid with advantage or disadvantage). This is deliberately not an arbitrary rules engine; a normal-mode zero-bonus entry is rejected. Bonuses from separate sources add to the original roll bonus without rewriting the statblock. Named advantage and disadvantage combine with conditions and manually chosen mode; any one of each cancels. Automatic condition failures take priority without producing fake dice. Each completed result names sources; cancelled or invalid dice remain explicit failures. Remove effects in bulk or per-statblock. Two identical monsters can have different effects.

**Clicked statblock links:** A rendered check, skill, save, initiative or attack link with a known d20 context and exact whole-number bonus uses the *clicked creature's own saved effects* and the real `Renderer.dice` log, whether or not that creature is selected for batch actions. SHIFT chooses advantage; CTRL/Command chooses disadvantage. An unsupported dynamic/compound d20 expression or a contextless d20 link shows a warning and retains the normal site roller; it is *not* reported as an effect-adjusted roll. Damage, recharge, non-d20 links, links outside the encounter and links on creatures without saved effects continue through the unmodified site roller. Attack effects apply to recognized clicked attack d20 links, not to an inferred attack from generic dice or damage. A statblock click does not change saved initiative; use **Roll selected initiative** to set turn-order totals.

### Verified preset catalog

The built-in search catalog is deliberately small: these five rules have explicit, verified mechanics and citations in this checkout. The source, page and edition of official presets are validated with their saved ID and mechanics; the house variant is explicitly not a book citation. Page citations refer to the particular rule, not another entry in the same file.

| Preset | Source / page | Eligibility and modeled scope | Unknown context |
|---|---|---|---|
| Desecrated Ground | DMG (2014) p. 110 | Undead standing there; advantage on saves only | Placement is explicitly assigned by the DM |
| Desecrated Ground **house rule** | House rule (no book page) | Undead standing there; advantage on saves and checks (including skills/initiative) | Placement is explicitly assigned by the DM |
| Heavy Precipitation | XDMG (2024) p. 69 | Wisdom (Perception) checks in heavy rain or snow; disadvantage | Prompt whether the creature is in the precipitation |
| Blizzard (ranged weapons) | IDRotF (2014) p. 10 | Ranged weapon attacks in a blizzard; disadvantage | Prompt whether this is a ranged weapon attack in the blizzard |
| Dark Blue Rhomboid Ioun Stone #016 | MECIounStones (installed homebrew) p. 18 | Owner's initiative and Wisdom (Perception) checks; advantage while bonded/orbiting | Prompt whether the stone is bonded and orbiting |

Catalog coverage was checked against representative local condition rules (`data/conditionsdiseases.json`), environmental hazards and adventures (`data/trapshazards.json`), spells (`data/spells/`), and installed homebrew (`homebrew/`). This is a curated catalog, **not** a parser of arbitrary prose or a claim to enumerate every rule in those files. Deliberate omissions include the blizzard's hearing/sight Perception clauses (sensory trigger and eye protection), PHB p. 290 Blinded's automatic failure on sight-dependent checks (sight context is not classified), XDMG p. 68 Extreme Heat's armor-specific save and fire-resistance auto-success, EGW p. 154 Gift of Alacrity's variable **1d8** initiative bonus (not a flat bonus), uncited homebrew entries, and narrative or DM-discretion effects. Damage, recharge, AC, resistances, rerolls, success thresholds, action restrictions, and contextual effects on *attacks against* a creature are not modeled.

**Edition differences:** The XPHB (2024) Surprised rule (p. 376) imposes initiative disadvantage; the PHB (2014) surprised rules do not use that 2024 modifier. XPHB (2024) Invisible (p. 370) includes initiative advantage; PHB (2014) Invisible (p. 291) has different attack-focused wording. The shared NPC condition engine does not infer edition from a saved condition name, so neither edition-specific initiative change is silently applied as a condition effect. Use a clearly named custom initiative effect only if its rule, edition, and trigger are known.

## Hit points and initiative

Each statblock now has independent **Current HP**, **Maximum HP**, **Temp HP**, and **Initiative** fields, including duplicate creatures. Current and maximum start from the monster's numeric `hp.average` (including zero). If the source has no usable numeric average, both are explicitly **Unset**, not zero; enter values directly. Temp starts at zero. Direct edits are saved independently, and a failed edit is restored on screen with an error. The roster displays each instance's own HP and initiative alongside its conditions/effects. The authored monster statblock is not rewritten when HP changes.

The focus bar's **Damage** shortcut opens **Apply typed damage** for the exact monsters selected in the roster, including members of different batches. Enter a positive amount or dice expression (rolled once for the batch), choose one of the standard damage types, and optionally identify a **Nonmagical attack**, **Magical attack**, or **Other effect**. Each instance uses its own *effective edited statblock* to apply immunity first, resistance (round down), then vulnerability, before damage consumes temporary HP and then current HP. An unspecified source does not silently resolve defenses that depend on how damage was dealt. For contextual, form-dependent, or unfamiliar defense wording, the page pauses the whole batch and asks whether each named defense applies to each target; nothing is saved until all decisions are supplied. Use **Apply reviewed damage** below the decisions (or the original Apply damage button above) to commit; changing the inputs or selection discards the pending review. Unset HP targets are reported and skipped; when all selected HP is unset, dice are not rolled. After applying, a bounded per-target report lists adjusted damage, defenses, HP/temp changes, and any suggested concentration-check DC (not rolled automatically). Saving failures leave HP and undo history unchanged. **Undo HP** also reverses a committed typed-damage batch, subject to the same safety checks and five-entry page-session history below.

**Selected monsters' HP** accepts a bare number or dice expression as damage, `-` as damage, `+` as healing, or `=` as a set value. For example, `8d6`, `-30`, `+12`, and `=15` all use the same selected targets. One dice expression is evaluated **once per batch** and that result is applied to every eligible instance. **Half damage** halves damage and rounds its magnitude down; it does not halve healing or an absolute set. Damage uses temporary HP first, then current HP (minimum zero); healing cannot raise current above maximum. A set value can exceed maximum. Monsters with unknown maximum HP are skipped; damage/healing also skips unknown current HP. The status names/counts skipped instances, and no dice are evaluated when no selected monster is eligible. **Undo HP** restores the last committed bulk operation, up to five recent operations in this page session; it is cleared on a direct HP edit or encounter replacement, and not restored after a page reload. A failed apply or undo does not change saved HP or consume an undo.

**Roll selected initiative** uses each monster's authored initiative bonus (including explicit monster bonuses) and rolls through the site's dice roller. Initiative is a Dexterity *ability check* for Poisoned/Frightened and broad check-scoped or initiative-scoped roll effects; the monster's authored initiative advantage also participates. Cancelled, unavailable, or invalid rolls do not overwrite an existing total. Successful selected rolls are saved together, with per-monster results and failures reported; the roster reflects saved totals immediately, even when a monster's statblock is not open. Initiative may also be entered/cleared manually on each statblock or edited inline in its turn entry.

**Turn order** lists monsters in descending saved whole-number initiative; equal totals keep original roster order. Drag an entry's **Move** button onto another turn (upper/lower half places before/after), or click **Move** and use the two position selectors, which also work with a keyboard or on touch. Unrolled monsters appear below the order and may be moved into it, including when the order is empty. A move changes the actual saved initiative total, not a separate rank: an available integer gap changes only the moved entry; a full gap adjusts the smallest necessary adjacent run. Every changed turn entry and old/new total is shown in the change list. **Undo last move** restores these totals in one step while initiative, group membership and turns remain unchanged; another initiative/group/turn edit or a reload removes that temporary undo. A failed save leaves the order and undo unchanged. Roster sorting remains a view choice, never an initiative edit. **Start turns** explicitly starts round 1 with the first rolled monster; **Next turn** advances and wraps to round 2, while **Reset turns** clears the active turn without erasing totals. A valid active turn keeps its stable ID and round when initiative order changes; clearing the active entry's initiative resets turns. Round and active ID survive reload.

**Share turns** is opt-in for a group of identical effective statblocks, whether the group was automatically shown or explicitly created. Enter one whole-number **Group total** or choose **Roll & share**, then confirm the displayed total and members' original totals (including unrolled members). A rolled group initiative uses the first member's roll effects/conditions; later **Roll selected initiative** rolls a shared group just once, using its first *selected* member, even when several are selected. The group has one canonical initiative/turn-order entry and **Next turn** advances past it once. Editing **Shared initiative** changes that canonical total, not the hidden individual totals. **Individual turns** restores every member's previous total, even after group rolls or manual changes. Group membership and its stable ID persist; distinct HP and conditions never merge. A mechanical edit that changes one member's effective statblock splits it out of an incompatible explicit group, retaining its HP, conditions, individual total and ID; the remaining shared turn stays active. Disbanding or disabling an active shared turn transfers the active marker to the first member with an original initiative, or explicitly resets turns if none has one. Splitting the last active member follows the same rule. Changes and any resulting active-turn reassignment are saved together and reported in the status message. View-group order is not turn order.

Working encounters are **v7**: v1 gains empty conditions/notes/effects, v2 retains conditions and gains empty notes/effects, and v3 retains all its effects. Only the two pre-existing v3/v4 Desecrated Ground presets can lack citation metadata; they gain canonical source/page/edition metadata in memory without a storage write. New presets require that metadata on load and save. Versions v1-v3 gain independent HP, unrolled initiative, and an unstarted turn; v1-v4 gain empty statblock histories; v1-v5 gain empty persistent groups and split exclusions; v1-v6 gain independent resource defaults from each effective statblock, all in memory without rewriting storage just to read it. Existing frozen snapshots, roll presets, notes, operations, IDs and selection survive migration. Replacing the encounter asks for confirmation and discards its statblock edits, HP, initiative, turns, groups, resources, conditions, area notes, roll effects, and target selection. A failed save leaves visible persisted state unchanged. The Bestiary saved list, pinned monsters, frozen snapshots, and DM Screen NPC Manager/Initiative Tracker state remain independent.

## One-time DM Screen handoff

Expand **Encounter setup & DM Screen handoff**, select the monsters to send and enter or roll an initiative total for **every** selected instance. **Queue selected monsters** creates a one-time snapshot in this browser. If one or more selected monsters have no initiative, the page names each missing instance and does not queue a partial roster. If a different snapshot is already queued, replacing it requires confirmation; **Clear queued snapshot** also requires confirmation. The queue displays the saved list name, monster count, and creation time. You can queue monsters while DM Screen is closed or has no Initiative Tracker panel yet.

Open DM Screen, add an **Initiative Tracker** panel if necessary, then use **Refresh queue** and **Import here** on the specific panel that should receive the monsters. Nothing imports automatically. That panel appends separate rows with each instance's name/number, a one-time copy of its **effective edited statblock**, current/maximum/temporary HP (including unset current or maximum), initiative total, and standard or custom condition names. Shared-turn members receive the chosen canonical total, not their saved original totals. The handoff format remains v1 and preserves separate per-instance identities (DM Screen does not receive shared-turn grouping). Locked or failed imports leave the queue available; after successful, verified persistence the queue is cleared. If the rows are visible but saving or queue clearing fails, reload DM Screen before trying again rather than importing duplicates. Each panel has its own **Clear queue** action; clearing never removes rows already imported. An unreadable queue cannot be replaced; use the separately confirmed **Clear damaged queue** recovery action instead.

The source working encounter, selected targets, Bestiary saved list, and pins are not modified. **Later HP, conditions, initiative, and turns never sync in either direction.** Area notes, manual lair-action reminders, and roll effects remain in Encounter Workspace because Initiative Tracker has no equivalent fields; they are disclosed before queuing/importing. The handoff uses same-origin browser storage and requires persistent storage and cross-tab locking. If those are unavailable, it reports the problem rather than claiming that a queue will survive reload. Directly editing DM Screen data in a second tab can still overwrite another tab's board state; close or reload stale tabs before continuing.

Batch attacks (no unambiguous attack to select), action economy, maps, and automatic lair actions remain out of scope.
