# Encounter Workspace

The separate [Encounter Workspace](../encounterworkspace.html) starts with a **saved Bestiary pinned list**, not the current pins or an uploaded list. Open it from the Bestiary's control bar (or its Encounter Builder settings when active) or the Dungeon Master navigation menu, then choose a saved list. A working copy is stored in this browser; neither the saved Bestiary list nor the DM Screen NPC Manager roster is changed.

Each counted creature becomes its own instance with a stable ID. Scaled CR, spell-summon, and class-summon variants are resolved using the pinned list's custom hash before a statblock snapshot is copied for each instance. The snapshot stays unchanged even if the Bestiary source or saved list later changes. All targets start selected; the roster checkboxes and **All/None** controls save individual target selection. Reloading restores the working encounter, edits, and selection without looking up the original source again.

During play, the combat strip stays above the roster and shows the current round/turn, active members' HP and conditions, target count, and **Start turns / Next turn / Reset turns**. The focused effective statblock opens on the active turn (or the first creature before turns begin); the grouped roster's **View** controls jump directly to any member, and **Previous / Next / Current turn** navigate without scrolling through statblocks. Roster View buttons support Up/Down arrow navigation. Explicit focus survives roster searches and sorting; advancing a turn focuses the new active member. **Encounter setup & DM Screen handoff** and **Selected monster actions** expand on demand; the latter contains the existing rolls, batch HP, conditions, initiative, turn order, bulk mechanical edits, and notes/effects. Nothing in those drawers changes what is stored just by opening them.

The roster search matches name (including numbered duplicates), source, CR, and condition; filters show conditioned, below-half-HP, defeated, or unrolled monsters. Sort by saved source order, descending initiative or CR, ascending current HP or HP percentage, name, or urgent status. Unset HP, unknown CR and unrolled initiative sort after known values; ties keep the original source order. Groups remain intact under filtering, with the header showing its full count and any smaller shown count. A group checkbox always targets the *full group*, including filtered-out members, and still shows a mixed state when only some are selected. The visible/total count and a no-match message make filters explicit. These are local view choices: IDs, saved targets, persistent groups, ties in canonical initiative, and the active turn never change when searching or sorting.

**Statblocks: Focused / All (load in batches)** changes only the view. Focused is the default and keeps the chosen creature available even if it falls outside the current roster filter. All shows the filtered roster's statblocks in batches of twelve, with separate expandable cards and **Focus** shortcuts. Closed cards do not render their full statblock; opening one loads its own effective edits, HP, conditions, notes/effects, and roll links, and closing it removes that full rendering. **Load more statblocks** reveals the next twelve card shells; a 1,000-instance encounter does not eagerly render 1,000 full statblocks.

The roster visually groups creatures with identical **effective statblocks**, including per-instance Quick Actions edits. Group identity does not depend on monster names, sources, or operation IDs: different edits that produce the same statblock group together, while different resulting statblocks separate. A group header gives the member count, an expand/collapse control, and a checkbox that selects or deselects all members; when only some are selected it shows a mixed state. Individual roster rows, IDs, labels, selection, HP, conditions, and statblock cards remain separate. Use **Split** on a member to keep it separate, **Rejoin** to restore compatible grouping, **Group selected** to persist a chosen subset of identical statblocks, or **Disband** to separate a persistent group. Splitting and persistent group membership survive reload; expand/collapse is a local view preference.

Choosing another saved list replaces the working copy only after confirmation (including when the current roster is empty). Missing homebrew, invalid scaling, and invalid counts are reported individually; available monsters still load. If no entries resolve, the encounter remains a named empty working copy with the load notices. The workspace does not write to Bestiary saved-list storage, and choosing or cancelling a list does not affect current pins.

To protect the browser from an unbounded roster, a saved list can materialize at most 1,000 instances. An entry that would exceed this limit is reported as omitted rather than partially materialized.

## Rolls and conditions

Expand **Selected monster actions** to choose an **ability check**, **saving throw**, or **skill check**, then an ability/skill and Normal, Advantage, or Disadvantage. **Roll selected** already applies saved roll effects: it rolls once for each selected instance through the site's dice roller and displays each monster's die, bonus, total, and contributing sources. Saving throws and skills use explicit monster bonuses where present, otherwise their governing ability modifier; installed skill definitions contribute their governing ability when available. Duplicate monsters are listed and rolled independently. Cancelling a roll or receiving an invalid dice result produces a failure row, not a made-up total; other selected monsters still complete.

**Conditions** applies/removes a chosen condition on the selected instances only. Each statblock shows its own removable condition chips, and the roster summarizes them. The picker uses site conditions and installed homebrew where available; conditions already in an encounter remain visible and removable if their source later disappears. Hover a known chip for its definition. Supported condition effects include Poisoned disadvantage on checks and attacks, Restrained disadvantage on Dexterity saves and attacks, and automatic failure of Strength and Dexterity saves for Paralyzed, Stunned, Unconscious, and Petrified. Encounter rolls ask whether Frightened's source is in sight and, for an Invisible attack, whether the target cannot see the attacker. If the prompt is dismissed, no die is rolled; answering No omits that conditional effect. A chosen advantage and condition disadvantage cancel (and vice versa); the result names both. No custom homebrew mechanics are inferred from condition text.

If condition/skill references cannot load, the page reports the error and keeps standard fallback choices available; saved Bestiary list import remains enabled when its sources initialized successfully.

Conditions and target selection are saved only on the encounter instances; the frozen monster snapshots and saved Bestiary list remain unchanged. Foundation v1 encounters load with empty conditions; check/save/skill rolls and their result table are transient, not persisted.

## Area notes and roll effects

Expand **Non-mechanical notes & roll effects** inside the selected actions; collapse it after preparation. Use **Area & lair reminders (text only)** to give selected monsters a named, plain-text area reminder or a **manual lair-action note**. These legacy notes remain non-mechanical reminders: they do not alter stats, appear as actions, trigger lair actions, or automate dice, and they are not converted into mechanical edits when an older encounter loads. A note applied to several targets is shown on every affected statblock, with a per-monster Remove button. Choose a note from **Applied note** to remove it from currently selected monsters in bulk; unselected instances, including duplicates of the same creature, keep their own copy. DM-authored names and descriptions are displayed as text, not parsed as HTML or game tags.

## Mechanical statblock edits

Use **Edit statblock** on a creature to open the same Quick Actions editor used by the Bestiary. Minion conversion, mechanical area traits, lair-action groups, magic items, and Quick Edit (core statistics and guided or Advanced JSON entries, including legendary and mythic actions) are available. Changes are saved as an operation history for **that instance**, not as an override keyed by monster name. Two copies of the same creature can have different edits; Remove and Clear All undo only the chosen instance's saved operations. The copied source snapshot and Bestiary saved list/pins never change. **Save to Homebrew** is separate and writes homebrew only if you explicitly choose it.

Expand **Bulk statblock edits** inside the selected actions to add a legendary action, choose a loaded lair-action group, apply an actual area trait, or convert to minions. The preview names the selected rule, lists eligible monsters, and names each skipped monster with its reason (for example, an existing action or an unsupported minion CR). Rule choices use their name and source, so reordering loaded homebrew cannot substitute another rule; a rule that disappears before preview is rejected instead. If confirmed, all eligible changes are saved in one write; a failed write leaves every target unchanged. If a save succeeds but refreshing the page fails, the notice says the changes were saved and asks you to reload rather than inviting a duplicate retry. A mechanical trait can modify the rendered statblock's speed, senses, defenses, HP, or entries according to its rule; unlike the free-text reminders above, these edits affect the creature the encounter actually uses.

When an edit changes the creature's effective `hp.average`, **that instance's** tracked current and maximum HP reset to the new usable numeric average (or Unset if none); temporary HP is retained. The confirmation/status reports the reset, and incompatible in-page HP undo entries are discarded. Other edits do not reset tracked HP. Rendered statblocks, batch and clicked-link rolls, skill options, and a one-time DM Screen handoff use the edited creature, while encounter roll-effect presets and notes remain separate.

**Roll effects** has a searchable, cited preset picker. Search a rule, book code, page, or description. A preset acts only on selected instances; choose only monsters that meet its stated area/item eligibility. The two Desecrated Ground variants skip non-undead by actual monster type and report skipped targets. Applying one replaces only the other Desecrated Ground variant, while other presets and custom effects can coexist. Presets with uncertain context prompt separately *at roll time* (Yes applies, No skips, dismissing cancels that instance's roll); applying the preset does not assert that a ranged attack, weather condition, or orbiting item is always active.

For other situations, give a **custom roll effect** a name/source, choose ability checks (the legacy broad scope also covers skills and initiative), skill checks only, saves, initiative only, and/or attacks; choose advantage/disadvantage/no mode change and enter a signed whole-number bonus (zero is valid with advantage or disadvantage). This is deliberately not an arbitrary rules engine; a normal-mode zero-bonus entry is rejected. Bonuses from separate sources add to the original roll bonus without rewriting the statblock. Named advantage and disadvantage combine with conditions and manually chosen mode; any one of each cancels. Automatic condition failures take priority without producing fake dice. Each completed result names sources; cancelled or invalid dice remain explicit failures. Remove effects in bulk or per-statblock. Two identical monsters can have different effects.

**Clicked statblock links:** A rendered check, skill, save, initiative or attack link with a known d20 context and exact whole-number bonus uses the *clicked creature's own saved effects* and the real `Renderer.dice` log, whether or not that creature is selected for batch actions. SHIFT chooses advantage; CTRL/Command chooses disadvantage. An unsupported dynamic/compound d20 expression or a contextless d20 link shows a warning and retains the normal site roller; it is *not* reported as an effect-adjusted roll. Damage, recharge, non-d20 links, links outside the encounter and links on creatures without saved effects continue through the unmodified site roller. Attack effects apply to recognized clicked attack d20 links, not to an inferred attack from generic dice or damage. A statblock click does not change saved initiative; use **Roll selected initiative** to set turn-order totals.

### Verified preset catalog

The built-in search catalog is deliberately small: these five rules have explicit, verified mechanics and citations in this checkout. The source, page and edition of official presets are validated with their saved ID and mechanics; the house variant is explicitly not a book citation. Page citations refer to the particular rule, not another entry in the same file.

| Preset | Source / page | Eligibility and modeled scope | Unknown context |
|---|---|---|---|
| Desecrated Ground | DMG (2014) p. 110 | Undead standing there; advantage on saves only | Placement is explicitly assigned by the DM |
| Desecrated Ground **house rule** | House rule (no book page) | Undead standing there; advantage on saves and checks (including skills/initiative) | Placement is explicitly assigned by the DM |
| Heavy Precipitation | XDMG (2024) p. 69 | Wisdom (Perception) checks in heavy rain or snow; disadvantage | Prompt whether the creature is in the precipitation |
| Blizzard (ranged weapons) | IDRotF (2014) p. 10 | Ranged weapon attacks in a blizzard; disadvantage | Prompt whether this is a ranged weapon attack in the blizzard |
| Dark Blue Rhomboid Ioun Stone #016 | MECIounStones (installed homebrew) p. 18 | Owner's initiative and Wisdom (Perception) checks; advantage while bonded/orbiting | Prompt whether the stone is bonded and orbiting |

Catalog coverage was checked against representative local condition rules (`data/conditionsdiseases.json`), environmental hazards and adventures (`data/trapshazards.json`), spells (`data/spells/`), and installed homebrew (`homebrew/`). This is a curated catalog, **not** a parser of arbitrary prose or a claim to enumerate every rule in those files. Deliberate omissions include the blizzard's hearing/sight Perception clauses (sensory trigger and eye protection), PHB p. 290 Blinded's automatic failure on sight-dependent checks (sight context is not classified), XDMG p. 68 Extreme Heat's armor-specific save and fire-resistance auto-success, EGW p. 154 Gift of Alacrity's variable **1d8** initiative bonus (not a flat bonus), uncited homebrew entries, and narrative or DM-discretion effects. Damage, recharge, AC, resistances, rerolls, success thresholds, action restrictions, and contextual effects on *attacks against* a creature are not modeled.

**Edition differences:** The XPHB (2024) Surprised rule (p. 376) imposes initiative disadvantage; the PHB (2014) surprised rules do not use that 2024 modifier. XPHB (2024) Invisible (p. 370) includes initiative advantage; PHB (2014) Invisible (p. 291) has different attack-focused wording. The shared NPC condition engine does not infer edition from a saved condition name, so neither edition-specific initiative change is silently applied as a condition effect. Use a clearly named custom initiative effect only if its rule, edition, and trigger are known.

## Hit points and initiative

Each statblock now has independent **Current HP**, **Maximum HP**, **Temp HP**, and **Initiative** fields, including duplicate creatures. Current and maximum start from the monster's numeric `hp.average` (including zero). If the source has no usable numeric average, both are explicitly **Unset**, not zero; enter values directly. Temp starts at zero. Direct edits are saved independently, and a failed edit is restored on screen with an error. The roster displays each instance's own HP and initiative alongside its conditions/effects. The authored monster statblock is not rewritten when HP changes.

**Selected monsters' HP** accepts a bare number or dice expression as damage, `-` as damage, `+` as healing, or `=` as a set value. For example, `8d6`, `-30`, `+12`, and `=15` all use the same selected targets. One dice expression is evaluated **once per batch** and that result is applied to every eligible instance. **Half damage** halves damage and rounds its magnitude down; it does not halve healing or an absolute set. Damage uses temporary HP first, then current HP (minimum zero); healing cannot raise current above maximum. A set value can exceed maximum. Monsters with unknown maximum HP are skipped; damage/healing also skips unknown current HP. The status names/counts skipped instances, and no dice are evaluated when no selected monster is eligible. **Undo HP** restores the last committed bulk operation, up to five recent operations in this page session; it is cleared on a direct HP edit or encounter replacement, and not restored after a page reload. A failed apply or undo does not change saved HP or consume an undo.

**Roll selected initiative** uses each monster's authored initiative bonus (including explicit monster bonuses) and rolls through the site's dice roller. Initiative is a Dexterity *ability check* for Poisoned/Frightened and broad check-scoped or initiative-scoped roll effects; the monster's authored initiative advantage also participates. Cancelled, unavailable, or invalid rolls do not overwrite an existing total. Successful selected rolls are saved together, with per-monster results and failures reported. Initiative may also be entered/cleared manually on each statblock.

**Turn order** lists rolled monsters in descending total order; equal totals keep original roster order. Unrolled monsters are listed as such, not inserted in the order. **Start turns** explicitly starts round 1 with the first rolled monster; **Next turn** advances to the next monster and wraps to the first in a new round. **Reset turns** clears the active turn and returns to round zero without erasing initiative. Changing an initiative total reorders entries but preserves the currently active monster; clearing that monster's initiative safely resets turns. Round and active monster survive a reload.

**Share turns** is opt-in for a group of identical effective statblocks, whether the group was automatically shown or explicitly created. Enter one whole-number **Group total** or choose **Roll & share**, then confirm the displayed total and members' original totals (including unrolled members). A rolled group initiative uses the first member's roll effects/conditions; later **Roll selected initiative** rolls a shared group just once, using its first *selected* member, even when several are selected. The group has one canonical initiative/turn-order entry and **Next turn** advances past it once. Editing **Shared initiative** changes that canonical total, not the hidden individual totals. **Individual turns** restores every member's previous total, even after group rolls or manual changes. Group membership and its stable ID persist; distinct HP and conditions never merge. A mechanical edit that changes one member's effective statblock splits it out of an incompatible explicit group, retaining its HP, conditions, individual total and ID; the remaining shared turn stays active. Disbanding or disabling an active shared turn transfers the active marker to the first member with an original initiative, or explicitly resets turns if none has one. Splitting the last active member follows the same rule. Changes and any resulting active-turn reassignment are saved together and reported in the status message. View-group order is not turn order.

Working encounters are **v6**: v1 gains empty conditions/notes/effects, v2 retains conditions and gains empty notes/effects, and v3 retains all its effects. Only the two pre-existing v3/v4 Desecrated Ground presets can lack citation metadata; they gain canonical source/page/edition metadata in memory without a storage write. New presets require that metadata on load and save. Versions v1-v3 gain independent HP, unrolled initiative, and an unstarted turn; v1-v4 gain empty statblock histories; v1-v5 gain empty persistent groups and split exclusions in memory, without rewriting storage just to read it. Existing frozen snapshots, roll presets, notes, operations, IDs and selection survive migration. Replacing the encounter asks for confirmation and discards its statblock edits, HP, initiative, turns, groups, conditions, area notes, roll effects, and target selection. A failed save leaves visible persisted state unchanged. The Bestiary saved list, pinned monsters, frozen snapshots, and DM Screen NPC Manager/Initiative Tracker state remain independent.

## One-time DM Screen handoff

Expand **Encounter setup & DM Screen handoff**, select the monsters to send and enter or roll an initiative total for **every** selected instance. **Queue selected monsters** creates a one-time snapshot in this browser. If one or more selected monsters have no initiative, the page names each missing instance and does not queue a partial roster. If a different snapshot is already queued, replacing it requires confirmation; **Clear queued snapshot** also requires confirmation. The queue displays the saved list name, monster count, and creation time. You can queue monsters while DM Screen is closed or has no Initiative Tracker panel yet.

Open DM Screen, add an **Initiative Tracker** panel if necessary, then use **Refresh queue** and **Import here** on the specific panel that should receive the monsters. Nothing imports automatically. That panel appends separate rows with each instance's name/number, a one-time copy of its **effective edited statblock**, current/maximum/temporary HP (including unset current or maximum), initiative total, and standard or custom condition names. Shared-turn members receive the chosen canonical total, not their saved original totals. The handoff format remains v1 and preserves separate per-instance identities (DM Screen does not receive shared-turn grouping). Locked or failed imports leave the queue available; after successful, verified persistence the queue is cleared. If the rows are visible but saving or queue clearing fails, reload DM Screen before trying again rather than importing duplicates. Each panel has its own **Clear queue** action; clearing never removes rows already imported. An unreadable queue cannot be replaced; use the separately confirmed **Clear damaged queue** recovery action instead.

The source working encounter, selected targets, Bestiary saved list, and pins are not modified. **Later HP, conditions, initiative, and turns never sync in either direction.** Area notes, manual lair-action reminders, and roll effects remain in Encounter Workspace because Initiative Tracker has no equivalent fields; they are disclosed before queuing/importing. The handoff uses same-origin browser storage and requires persistent storage and cross-tab locking. If those are unavailable, it reports the problem rather than claiming that a queue will survive reload. Directly editing DM Screen data in a second tab can still overwrite another tab's board state; close or reload stale tabs before continuing.

Batch attacks (no unambiguous attack to select), action economy, maps, and automatic lair actions remain out of scope.
